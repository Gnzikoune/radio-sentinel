# Repurposing a $0 3G USB Dongle into an Autonomous Cellular Wardriving Station (Radio Sentinel)

*Ready-to-publish article for Hackaday.io, Dev.to, Medium, or Hacker News Show HN.*

---

## The Origin: What to do with forgotten 3G USB modems?

Most hackers and engineers have at least one old 3G USB modem dongle sitting in a junk drawer. With 4G and 5G dominating modern networks, these devices are widely considered e-waste. Yet inside lies a complete 3GPP-compliant cellular baseband processor, a digital transceiver, and an AT command interpreter.

**Radio Sentinel** was born from a simple challenge: *Can we repurpose an off-the-shelf, carrier-locked Alcatel 3G USB stick into an autonomous cellular RF observatory and rogue base station detector for $0?*

The answer is yes. Paired with an ordinary Android smartphone as a high-frequency satellite and 4G QoS telemetry probe, Radio Sentinel provides real-time RF heatmapping, base station triangulation, dual-network performance auditing, and 2G downgrade attack (IMSI-catcher) alerts.

Here is an architectural breakdown of the build.

---

## 1. Hardware Architecture

* **Repurposed Modem:** Alcatel OneTouch X602D (*TCT Mobile*, Spreadtrum SC7702 chipset, USB ID `1BBB:022C`).
* **Mobile Sensor Probe:** Samsung Galaxy A14 running standard Android Chrome (no native mobile app required).
* **Host Station:** Lightweight Python 3.10+ FastAPI backend serving a responsive cockpit dashboard over local HTTPS.

---

## 2. Low-Level Reverse Engineering: The Hayes Command Pipeline

Instead of running bloated proprietary connection managers, Radio Sentinel opens a direct raw serial connection (`115,200 baud`) to the composite virtual COM port.

Using standard 3GPP AT commands:
* **`AT+CSQ`**: Reads raw RSSI, instantly mapped to real physical milliwatts ($\text{dBm} = 2 \times \text{CSQ} - 113$).
* **`AT+COPS?`**: Extracts Public Land Mobile Network (PLMN) identity (MCC and MNC).
* **`AT+CREG?`**: Tracks network registration state.
* **`AT+CCED=0,1`**: Extracts deep engineering topology: serving **Cell ID**, **Location Area Code (LAC)**, and **UARFCN carrier channel** (used to dynamically derive carrier frequency in MHz for UMTS Band 1 / 8).
* **SIM-less Emergency Mode**: In accordance with international telecommunications standards, modems listen to Broadcast Control Channels (BCCH) even when no SIM card is present in order to route emergency calls (112/911). Radio Sentinel uses this mechanism for 100% passive carrier surveillance.

---

## 3. Smartphone Integration: Dual-Network Auditing & Zero-App Telemetry

Rather than buying an external GPS USB dongle:
1. The smartphone scans an on-screen **Smart QR Code** generated by the dashboard.
2. The HTML5 Geolocation API (`watchPosition`) streams high-precision GNSS coordinates directly to the backend.
3. The browser's `NetworkInformation` API streams live 4G cellular telemetry: estimated downlink throughput (Mbps), RTT latency, and server ping.

### Physical vs Data Layer Diagnostic
When the USB stick listens passively to Operator A while the phone has an active SIM on Operator B, the platform correlates:
- **Core Network Saturation**: High RF power ($\ge -85\text{ dBm}$) with spiking RTT ($> 250\text{ ms}$) $\rightarrow$ carrier IP backbone congestion.
- **RF Obstacle Attenuation**: Simultaneous drop in both dBm and throughput $\rightarrow$ physical fading or terrain masking.

---

## 4. Cyber-Defense: Catching 2G Downgrade Attacks

IMSI-catchers (Stingrays) frequently exploit protocol weaknesses by forcing nearby handsets down from authenticated 3G/4G down to unencrypted 2G GSM (or weak A5/1 ciphering), combined with abnormally high transmission power.

Radio Sentinel continuously checks modulation transitions:
* Detecting a sudden drop from 3G/4G to 2G GSM accompanied by a power spike ($\ge +12\text{ dBm}$) or unexpected LAC switch triggers an immediate critical visual and acoustic alert.

---

## 5. Geospatial Heatmaps & 3D Google Earth KML

* **Real-Power Centroid Triangulation**: Converts logarithmic dBm into linear milliwatts ($10^{\text{dBm}/10}\text{ mW}$) to calculate the weighted center of mass for base station localization.
* **OpenCellID Registry Integration**: Validates observed Cell IDs against official cell registries (with a Haversine sanity filter).
* **3D Volumetric Google Earth Export**: Generates continuous extruded ribbon tracks in KML where point height reflects RF signal strength.
